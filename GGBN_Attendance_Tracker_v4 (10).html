Safety Information
Critical safety guidelines, contacts, and attendance thresholds

Attendance Safety Thresholds

▸Members below 60% attendance must be flagged immediately. Excused absences count as full credit.
▸Three or more consecutive unexcused absences require a welfare check and written report to the PL.
▸Excused absences must be submitted in advance or within 24 hours. Late excusals become Absent.
▸All attendance records are subject to audit. Falsifying records is a serious violation.
